Our approach

Why don't we
make the liquor ourselves?

"Good distribution is a craft too."

There is plenty of infused liquor sold online in Vietnam, but buyers can rarely tell which bottle is traceable and which is spiked with industrial alcohol or colouring.

Viet Distillery does not distil. Our job is to choose producers with complete paperwork, check every order and deliver it to you, so all you do is pour.

FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

How strong is it?

The fruit flavours and Herbal Leaf are around 18-20% ABV, lighter than Vietnamese rice spirit (35-40%). The exact figure is printed on each label. It is still alcohol, so pour small and sip slowly.

Which flavour is easiest for a first try?

Apricot: gently sweet and the easiest to drink. For clearly sour, choose Salted Apricot; lightly tart, Plum; richer and sweeter, Mulberry. Not sure? The 4-litre combo lets you try all four.

How should I drink it?

Chilled, in a small glass, is when the sweet-sour taste is clearest. Over ice it is lighter and lasts longer. Salted Apricot with soda and a slice of lime is great on a hot day.

Any colourings or flavourings?

No. The colour comes from the infused fruit, so slight variation between batches is normal.

Shipping cost and delivery time?

Free nationwide, no minimum. Ho Chi Minh City: within 24 hours, next day at the latest. Other provinces go by courier, and we tell you the expected date when we confirm your order.

Can I inspect the parcel before paying?

Yes. Open it, check the bottles and the tax stamp, then pay the courier. If it is not as described, hand it back and pay nothing.

Can I pay by card or from abroad?

Payment is cash on delivery in Vietnamese dong. We deliver within Vietnam only.

How do I store it?

Somewhere cool and out of direct sunlight. Once opened, keep it in the fridge.

Who makes it?

Apricot, Plum, Salted Apricot and Herbal Leaf are Núi Mường liquor by Thủy Phương Private Enterprise (Lào Cai City), with production licence No. 85/GP-PKT, a Quatest 1 lab report and product declarations. Mulberry is Việt Thành Mulberry liqueur by Việt Thành Agricultural Service Cooperative (Trấn Yên, Yên Bái), a sister producer with its own product declaration. Viet Distillery is the distributor and does not make the liquor.

Do you sell to under-18s?

No. We sell only to people aged 18 or over and may refuse delivery if the recipient is under age.
